# Retail Insights - E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes SuperStore sales data to uncover meaningful business insights related to revenue, profitability, product performance, customer segments, and regional sales trends.

The objective of this project is to perform end-to-end data analysis by cleaning the dataset, conducting exploratory data analysis (EDA), creating interactive Power BI dashboards, and providing strategic business recommendations to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Project Objectives

- Clean and preprocess sales data.
- Identify sales and profit trends.
- Analyze category and sub-category performance.
- Evaluate regional and market performance.
- Study the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

## Key Findings

### Business Performance

- Total Revenue: **$12.64 Million**
- Total Profit: **$1.47 Million**
- Total Orders: **25,035**
- Total Customers: **795**
- Profit Margin: **11.62%**

### Category Insights

- Technology is the highest revenue-generating category.
- Technology also contributes the highest profit.
- Furniture has the lowest profit margin among major categories.
- Tables is the largest loss-making sub-category.

### Regional Insights

- Central Region generates the highest revenue.
- North Asia has one of the strongest profit margins.
- Southeast Asia shows high sales volume but low profitability.

### Product Insights

#### Top Performing Product

- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier

#### Major Loss-Making Product

- Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head

### Market Insights

- APAC is the highest revenue-generating market.
- Canada is the smallest market by revenue.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

### Optimize Discount Strategy

Reduce excessive discounting on low-margin products to improve profitability.

### Focus on Technology Products

Increase inventory and marketing efforts for high-performing technology products.

### Improve Southeast Asia Performance

Investigate operational costs and discount policies in Southeast Asia.

### Review Loss-Making Products

Reassess pricing strategies and product portfolio for consistently loss-making products.

### Seasonal Planning

Increase inventory and marketing efforts during peak sales periods in Q3 and Q4.
